# Sema positioning experiment

Prepared 2026-09-18 from `docs/brand/SEMA_COPY_BRIEF.md` and the current checkout.
The actual landing app is `web/apps/landing`; there is no root `/landing` directory.

## Variant A

- **Positioning hypothesis:** recognition of recurring semantic integration work earns an engineering lead's attention. The valuable change is retaining the reasoning so another source does not require reconstructing the whole project.
- **Hero thesis:** “The next source. The same mapping project?”
- **Primary engineering pain:** codes, joins, cardinality, and mapping rationale are scattered across spreadsheets, tickets, SQL, and people. Changes reopen settled questions.
- **Investor takeaway:** mapping, reconciliation, and exception review are existing engineering/services work that could become repeatable software. The decision record is what can accumulate.
- **Main claims:** explicit source-to-target records; implemented cBioPortal/OMOP vocabulary fitting; schema-scoped graph builds; persisted correction events and bounded replay support; inspectable provenance; filtered downstream context.
- **Human verification:** PostgreSQL deployment status; a publishable measured showcase; pilot capacity, target scope, and duration; privacy and contact destinations; production waitlist behavior. Broader automatic refitting and model proposal are not presented as shipped.

## Variant B

- **Positioning hypothesis:** distrust of plausible semantic inference is the strongest entry point. Readers want infrastructure to distinguish a proposal from a decision with evidence and a recorded status.
- **Hero thesis:** “Understanding got cheap. Trust didn't.”
- **Primary engineering pain:** unchecked interpretations propagate through joins and queries, while corrections disappear into prompts or comments and cannot reliably inform the next run.
- **Investor takeaway:** the durable asset is the accumulated, corrected decision record. A model call creates a candidate; repeated expert adjudication is the workflow to productize.
- **Main claims:** provenance and confidence; accepted/rejected/unresolved distinctions; vocabulary domain rules and required-field checks; persisted human status events; bounded correction replay; status/confidence-filtered context.
- **Human verification:** the same facts as A, so evidence quality and maturity do not become hidden experimental variables. No universal approval-only downstream guarantee or automatic correction preservation is claimed.

## Key differences

1. **Entry emotion:** A starts with fatigue at repeating integration work; B starts with skepticism toward a plausible mapping.
2. **Unit of wasted effort:** A centers the integration project and the lost reason behind a mapping. B centers the adjudication that turns an interpretation into something usable.
3. **Narrative failure:** A's mapping knowledge is hard to recover when a source changes. B's correction exists but is disconnected from the next inference.
4. **Product mechanism hierarchy:** A leads with inspecting sources and fitting codes to a target. B leads with separating inference from acceptance, then explains the same fitting mechanics.
5. **Operational payoff:** A emphasizes continuity between source onboarding tasks. B emphasizes inspecting uncertainty and the evidence behind downstream context.
6. **Proof interpretation:** A's refusal is an integration task with a recorded reason. B's refusal demonstrates that target rules can outweigh a plausible suggestion. Both show the same fixtures.
7. **Why now:** A treats inexpensive inference as a way to address the mapping backlog; B treats it as the reason decision governance matters more as agents reuse interpretations.
8. **Compounding thesis:** A asks whether the next source can reuse previous integration work; B asks whether previous human judgments become reusable evidence.
9. **Pilot evaluation:** A asks which decisions the next source reuses. B asks which interpretations have adequate evidence. The source requirements and proposed deliverables are held constant.

## Deliberately softened or omitted

- PostgreSQL is included at the owner's request, explicitly **coming soon**, not claimed shipped. Expected 1–2 day timing is recorded here rather than turned into an aging homepage deadline.
- “Only the new part gets fitted” becomes schema-scoped graph building. The code does not establish arbitrary incremental semantic refitting or change-triggered maintenance.
- “Survives every rebuild” becomes persisted correction events plus matching replay support and an explicit preservation workflow. Neither universal correction transfer nor complete automatic history preservation is verified.
- “Every decision keeps who changed it” becomes actor/time where supported. Named reviewer identity is not guaranteed by the status-event actor field.
- “Never guesses” and “only approved context” become status/confidence filtering. Automatic assertions may pass; NO_MAP and review-pending data can still appear in staging.
- “Sema checks its own work” becomes vocabulary/domain checks and required-field/review verdicts. Cardinality appears as an integration pain, not an invented blocked showcase decision or comprehensive validator claim.
- “Unified tables in your warehouse” is bounded to physical output in the OMOP workflow; generic unification and complete target conformance are not promised.
- “Row data stays where it is” is removed because prompt assembly can contain sampled rows. No security certification, automatic PII redaction, or zero-egress promise is added.
- No general-purpose model proposal, arbitrary-source fitting, packaged integration with a semantic platform, MCP product promise, or metric engine promise is introduced. Those are not needed to establish either positioning hypothesis.
- No patient totals, duplicate-patient merger counts, performance numbers, mapping accuracy, or dated three-study result is claimed. The repository contains synthetic POC counts on another branch and provisional evaluation guidance, not a reviewed artifact establishing the control's proposed cancer-study claims. Those counts are not substituted as evidence.
- No “nothing cancer-specific” assertion: healthcare few-shot examples and OMOP policy modules exist.
- No fundraising stage, paid-pilot traction, fixed delivery time, available-slot count, or fabricated contact address. Investor and footer links lead to existing sections instead of empty `mailto:` links.
